# Adoption and Change Management Playbook

## Objective

Ensure the deployed system fits the human organization, preserves accountability, and achieves sustained use.

## Stakeholder analysis

For each stakeholder record:

- role and influence
- current responsibilities
- expected workflow change
- perceived benefits
- perceived threats
- training needs
- decision authority
- communication needs

## Human-in-the-loop design

Assess:

- review volume
- review complexity
- acceptable turnaround
- reviewer qualification
- approval fatigue
- escalation burden
- disagreement handling
- accountability
- coverage during absence

## Adoption plan

Include:

1. sponsor narrative and business purpose
2. user involvement in design and evaluation
3. pilot cohort
4. training by role
5. clear limitation and escalation guidance
6. feedback channel
7. office hours or support
8. adoption metrics
9. recognition of workflow changes
10. post-launch review

## Trust practices

- show evidence and source references
- expose uncertainty and confidence appropriately
- explain why human review is requested
- preserve user correction and override
- demonstrate audit trails
- communicate incidents transparently
- avoid overstating model capability

## Adoption metrics

- active users
- eligible cases processed
- recommendation acceptance
- edit and override rates
- escalation rates
- review time
- user satisfaction
- support requests
- avoided or repeated manual work

## Outputs

- stakeholder impact map
- responsibility matrix
- training plan
- communication plan
- human-review staffing plan
- feedback and adoption dashboard specification

## Quality checks

- Did affected users participate in design?
- Is review volume operationally sustainable?
- Are incentives aligned?
- Are role and accountability changes explicit?
- Is the system usable during exceptions and outages?
