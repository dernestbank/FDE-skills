# Title: Problem Signature and Effective Pattern

## Summary

One paragraph explaining the reusable learning and why it matters.

## Problem signature

Observable conditions indicating this pattern applies.

## Context and constraints

Relevant systems, workflow characteristics, risk, data, human, and organizational conditions.

## Root cause

The mechanism that created the problem. Separate confirmed cause from remaining hypotheses.

## Failed or weaker approaches

What was tried or considered, why it underperformed, and evidence.

## Effective intervention

The process, deterministic, AI, agent, human-control, or deployment pattern that worked.

## Implementation notes

Contracts, sequence, controls, tooling, or operational details necessary to reuse the pattern.

## Validation

How the outcome was tested or observed. Label measured and estimated results.

## Applicability

Prerequisites and environments where the pattern is likely useful.

## Contraindications

Conditions where the pattern should not be applied.

## Failure and drift signals

Signals that the pattern is no longer working or the environment has changed.

## Security, human, and operational considerations

Important permissions, data, adoption, workload, incident, or rollback implications.

## Related knowledge

Links to source engagements, reviews, pulse reports, concepts, and related patterns.
