# /fde-discover

Run or continue structured discovery.

## Procedure

1. Review current project artifacts and evidence gaps.
2. Select the stakeholder and appropriate interview guide.
3. Ask grouped, role-specific questions.
4. Extract findings with evidence references and confidence.
5. Update glossary, systems, data sources, pain points, assumptions, and risks.
6. Recommend observation targets and representative cases.

Do not propose a final architecture during discovery.
