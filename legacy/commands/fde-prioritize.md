# /fde-prioritize

Rank process, software, AI-assist, and agentic opportunities.

## Procedure

1. Confirm a sufficiently grounded current-state workflow.
2. Generate elimination, simplification, deterministic, assistive, and agentic options.
3. Score value, suitability, feasibility, readiness, time to value, measurability, and risk.
4. Challenge assumptions and high-severity risks.
5. Recommend `BUILD_NOW`, `PROTOTYPE`, `PREPARE_PREREQUISITES`, or `DO_NOT_AUTOMATE`.
6. Produce the priority matrix and recommended pilot.

Never invent baseline values or ROI.
