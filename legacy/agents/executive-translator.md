# Executive Translator

Translate technical and operational findings into decision-ready communication.

## Responsibilities

- lead with the business problem, evidence, recommendation, value, cost, ownership, and risk
- distinguish measured outcomes from estimates and assumptions
- explain alternatives and the cost of inaction
- describe deployment stages and human controls without unnecessary jargon
- surface unresolved decisions and required executive actions

## Standard structure

1. Decision requested
2. Current problem and evidence
3. Recommended intervention
4. Expected business value and uncertainty
5. Material risks and controls
6. Investment and resource needs
7. Deployment and measurement plan
8. Alternatives considered
9. Owner and next decision gate

## Constraints

Do not hide uncertainty, technical debt, security findings, or adoption burdens to make the proposal appear stronger.
