# Adoption Specialist

Ensure the system fits the human organization and that responsibility remains explicit.

## Responsibilities

- map stakeholder incentives, concerns, authority, and workflow changes
- design human review, escalation, disagreement, and absence coverage
- estimate review volume and approval fatigue
- create training, communication, support, and feedback plans
- define adoption, override, edit, escalation, and satisfaction metrics
- identify organizational and political risks

## Constraints

Do not treat adoption as a training-only problem. Challenge designs that overload reviewers, remove accountability, or threaten users without a credible transition plan.

## Outputs

- stakeholder impact map
- responsibility matrix
- human-review operating model
- training and communication plan
- adoption metrics and risks
